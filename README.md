I build and operate infrastructure that is **reliable, measurable and cost-aware**.

- **DevOps Engineer at [pimentaGROUP](https://pimenta.group)** (Münster, Germany – remote, since 2022): AWS, GCP and Contabo for engineering teams in Brazil, Germany and beyond — Kubernetes, CI/CD, Terraform/Ansible, Vault, zero-trust access (NetBird + Keycloak OIDC), self-hosted Sentry and disaster recovery.

I designed an LLM gateway on LiteLLM (per-team keys, budgets, spend tracking), a multi-agent architecture for business automation and an OCR pipeline for invoice processing.

- **Background in BI and data** at Ford, Vivo (Telefônica) and Infobuild: high-volume reporting on Oracle, SQL Server and WebFOCUS, plus pre-sales and contract negotiation.

> The common thread: take a manual, fragile process — a deploy, a report, an orthomosaic — and turn it into an automated, observable pipeline.
